## Steps to add a YouTube Video
1. Go to the Files/Assets page in your OptiSigns account.
2. Click on the "App" button and select "YouTube" from the list of available apps.
3. Enter the URL of the YouTube video, playlist, or live stream you want to display.
4. (Optional) Configure settings like Volume, Auto-play, and Mute.
5. Click Save to add the YouTube app to your assets.
